Our approach

Keep it
moving.

THE WORKOUTS is a 24/7 community gym in G-13/2, Islamabad. It is designed around the practical stuff that makes a routine stick: access when you need it, coaching when you ask for it, and a floor where members help each other.

Fees are kept grounded. The gym uses fans rather than air conditioning — a deliberate tradeoff that helps keep training affordable. The atmosphere is premium in confidence, not in distance.
